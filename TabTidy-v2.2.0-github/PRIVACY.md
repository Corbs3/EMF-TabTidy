# TabTidy Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 2026**

## Summary

TabTidy does **not** collect, send or share any of your data. Everything happens inside your browser.

## What TabTidy reads

- **Tab addresses and titles** in your current window, so it can group tabs by website, apply your group rules and spot duplicate tabs
- This only happens when:
  - you open the TabTidy popup
  - your browser starts, if you have switched on "Tidy when the browser starts"
  - auto tidy runs, if you have switched it on (every 15 minutes to every 4 hours, as you choose)

## What TabTidy stores

Stored only on your device using Chrome's local storage:

- Your settings (for example, how often auto tidy runs and when it last ran)
- Your categories (names, colours, websites and whether each is on or off)
- Saved sessions (group names, colours and the addresses of the tabs in each group, up to 10 sessions)

While the browser is open, TabTidy also remembers which group it made for which website, so auto tidy can add new tabs to it. This is cleared when the browser closes.

You can delete saved sessions and rules at any time from the popup. Removing the extension deletes all of this data.

## What TabTidy changes

- It groups, ungroups, renames and closes tabs **only when you ask it to** (for example, pressing "Close extras" closes duplicate tabs)
- If you switch on auto tidy, it adds ungrouped tabs to groups on your chosen timer. It never closes tabs on its own

## What TabTidy never does

- No data is sent to any server
- No analytics, tracking or advertising
- No data is sold or shared with anyone

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| `tabs` | To read tab addresses and titles so tabs can be grouped and duplicates found |
| `tabGroups` | To create, name, colour and collapse tab groups |
| `storage` | To save your settings, group rules and sessions on your device |
| `alarms` | To run auto tidy on the timer you choose |
